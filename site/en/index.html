Concept validation and incubation

Ventures born from real mountain problems

We work with ideas and teams at their most fragile stage. First we test that the problem exists, that the solution holds and that the team can carry it. Then we incubate the concepts that pass, until they are ready for a first pilot in the field.

The programmeVentures

The problem

Mountain problems are concrete: slopes that move, water to manage, infrastructure to monitor, heritage to recover. The technical skills to tackle them are often already there, in the valleys’ companies and people.

What is missing is the step from a promising idea to a venture someone can invest time and money in. Without that step many ideas stall halfway, or start without the foundations to grow.

What we do

Phase 1

Concept validation

We test the problem, the solution and the team. The outcome is a clear decision: go ahead, change course or stop.

Phase 2

Incubation

For validated concepts: business model, structure, governance and partners, until the venture is ready for its first pilot.

Grow in the mountains, work with the plains

The mountains have the technical skills and the capacity to execute. The ventures we validate grow there, with the people of the territory, and from there serve clients and attract talent from across the Veneto.

Our base is the Veneto, starting from Belluno.

How we choose

Rooted in the territory

A real operational link to the mountains. The territory is where the problem comes from and the first place the solution is tested.

Ambition beyond the valley

Water, climate risk and infrastructure resilience are Alpine problems with much wider relevance. We look for solutions that can grow beyond the local market.

Ready to build together

We work inside the venture, alongside the founders. That needs a team willing to challenge its own assumptions.

Have an idea born from a mountain problem?

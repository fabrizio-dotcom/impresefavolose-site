The programme

Two phases, each closed by an explicit decision. We work before the pilot, where a good test costs little and is worth a lot.

  1. 1

    Phase 1 · Gate 1

    Concept validation

    • Is the territorial problem real, and does someone feel it enough to pay for a solution?
    • Does the technical approach hold up with people who know the field?
    • Can the team execute, or does it know who it is missing?

    At Gate 1 we look for grounded conviction. Traction is measured later. The decision is one of three: go ahead, change course, stop.

  2. 2

    Phase 2 · Gate 2

    Incubation

    • Business model and assumptions to test in the field.
    • Company structure and governance ready for partners and investors.
    • Technical and territorial partners for the pilot.
    • Pilot plan: where, with whom, which data to collect.

    Gate 2 closes incubation: the venture has the partners, site, plan and governance to start its first pilot.

After Gate 2

The pilot and the growth belong to the venture, with its own partners and investors. Imprese Favolose can stay alongside as an advisor or on the board.

Governance from day one

Every gate is a real decision, taken against criteria known in advance, and written down.

Conflicts of interest

Whoever assesses a venture gains no commercial advantage from it. Possible conflicts are declared and managed through a written assessment framework.

Confidentiality

Founders’ information stays protected. Separation rules between partners (Chinese walls) stop anyone using it for their own advantage.

Clear roles

Fab Campaigns Ltd supports Imprese Favolose as an advisor and in its governance, with no executive roles in the ventures.
